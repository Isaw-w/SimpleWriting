SimpleWriting

A writing app that never writes for you.

A minimalist, distraction-free macOS editor with no AI ghostwriter. It won't autocomplete your sentences or rewrite your paragraphs. The one thing it does for you is check your grammar — and even then it only points at the mistake and names the rule. You make the fix.

Download for macOS  ·  Source on GitHub  ·  free, MIT, macOS 13+

Philosophy

You do the writing. You keep the agency. You produce your best possible piece.

Most modern tools now finish your thoughts for you. That is convenient — and it is also how you stop being a writer. SimpleWriting takes the opposite stance. The words on the page are yours, start to finish. It is for people learning English, practising their craft, or who simply believe writing is worth doing yourself.

What it does, and doesn't

  • No text generation. No autocomplete, no “rewrite this,” no suggestions to accept. Nothing writes for you.
  • Grammar that teaches. It highlights the exact wrong word and explains the rule — never the corrected sentence. You fix it, so you learn it.
  • It trusts your voice. Register, style, and intentional phrasing are left alone. It flags real errors, not your personality.
  • Local and private. Drafts live on your disk. Your API key stays in your own settings. Nothing goes to the cloud but the grammar request you choose.
  • On-device style hints. Long sentences, adverbs, passive voice and qualifiers are highlighted locally — no key required.
  • Minimal by design. One window. Drafts on the left, your writing on the right. Nothing else asking for attention.

How the grammar check works

It is deliberately conservative and explain-only. It marks the smallest wrong span — a single word or short phrase, never a whole sentence — and gives you the rule, not the rewrite:

“The subject is singular, so the verb should be singular.”
— not “change give to gives.”

Checks run at temperature 0, so the same text yields the same result — no flickering, no errors that come and go. Grammar works with any OpenAI-compatible API (DeepSeek, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, or a local model). Leave it blank and SimpleWriting is still a fine distraction-free editor.

Install

  1. Download SimpleWriting.app, then right-click it → Open → Open the first time.
  2. Open Settings (⌘ ,) and add your OpenAI-compatible Base URL, Model, and API Key.

Or build it yourself:

git clone https://github.com/Isaw-w/SimpleWriting.git
cd SimpleWriting
./build.sh
open SimpleWriting.app
